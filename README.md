# Hi, I'm Hongjie 👋

![Hongjie AI Workbench](./assets/portfolio-terminal.svg)

I build practical AI products at the intersection of **product thinking, agent systems, and cross-border credit risk**.

My work turns complex workflows into usable systems—from multi-agent risk analysis and policy RAG pipelines to playful consumer AI products. I care about products that are explainable, testable, and ready to use.

---

## ⭐ Featured AI & risk systems

🤖 [**creditpulse-ai**](https://github.com/hongjieshi82-crypto/creditpulse-ai)  
A multi-agent system for cross-border credit risk. It calculates core portfolio metrics, detects abnormal changes, retrieves regulatory context, and generates risk reports.

📚 [**cross-border-credit-policy-rag**](https://github.com/hongjieshi82-crypto/cross-border-credit-policy-rag)  
A RAG evaluation pipeline for international credit policy and compliance documents, with metadata filtering, source tracing, and hallucination-resistant answers.

🛡️ [**CreditPulse-RiskGuard**](https://github.com/hongjieshi82-crypto/CreditPulse-RiskGuard)  
A multilingual financial risk content-control system combining few-shot prompting, reasoning audits, and deterministic validation.

🧭 [**ai-product-workbench**](https://github.com/hongjieshi82-crypto/ai-product-workbench)  
A product-management workspace for goals, requirements, tasks, roadmaps, bad cases, and case clustering.
